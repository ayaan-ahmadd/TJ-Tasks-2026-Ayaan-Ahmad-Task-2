About Pulaowala

Our Story

About Pulaowala Founded in December 2023 on 40 Foot Road, Shaheen Bagh, New Delhi, Pulaowala brings the authentic and comforting flavors of Bihar to your plate. From our signature Bihari Pulao to flavorful Chicken, Mutton, and Bihari Kebabs, every dish is prepared with traditional flavors and care.

With 100,000+ orders served, we’re proud to be a part of countless meals and memories. Our mission is simple serve authentic Bihari food that feels like home.
